Raahi is an enterprise carpooling platform that helps employees from registered organizations discover and share rides. It supports finding rides, offering rides, managing trips, live trip tracking, payments, ride history, reports, analytics, vehicle management, company administration, saved places, help, support, and communication between ride participants.
